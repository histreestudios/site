07 👀 Why attention matters

Attention is the
real currency.

Every inbox, every DM, every comment section is full of people fighting for the same eyes. If your message doesn't earn attention in the first line, nothing else you wrote matters - it's never read.

What it means

Attention = the prospect choosing to keep reading instead of scrolling past.

Why it's scarce

They get dozens of pitches a week. Generic = invisible.

How you earn it

Be specific about THEM, not about you. Specificity is what stops the scroll.

09 🗣️ Word play - sales terms, part 1

Speak the language. 🧠

These words get thrown around in every sales conversation. Know them cold.

🎯 LeadAny person or business who could possibly become a client.
✅ ProspectA lead you've qualified - they actually fit your ICP.
🧊 Cold LeadSomeone who has never interacted with you or your brand before.
🔥 Warm LeadSomeone who already engaged with you - replied, commented, liked, watched.
📧 Cold EmailThe first message to someone who doesn't know you, sent to start a conversation.
💬 Cold DMSame as cold email, but sent through Instagram/LinkedIn/Twitter direct messages.
📈 ROIReturn on Investment - what you got back compared to what you spent.
🤝 AcquisitionThe process of turning a stranger into a paying client.
💰 CACCustomer Acquisition Cost - how much it costs, on average, to win one client.
10 🗣️ Word play - sales terms, part 2

The pitch words. 🎯

💎 LTVLifetime Value - total revenue one client brings you over the whole relationship.
🔁 Conversion RateThe % of people who take the next step - reply, book a call, sign the deal.
🚰 PipelineEvery lead you're working right now, sorted by stage - new, warm, negotiating, closed.
🧭 ICPIdeal Client Profile - the exact type of person or business you serve best.
😣 Pain PointThe real problem or frustration the prospect feels right now.
🌟 Desired OutcomeThe result the prospect actually wants, once the pain is gone.
👉 CTACall To Action - the exact next step you're asking them to take.
📤 OutboundYou reach out first - emails, DMs, comments.
📥 InboundThey come to you first - through content, referrals, SEO, word of mouth.
12 📤📥 Outbound vs inbound

Two roads to the
same client.

OUTBOUND

We go find them

Cold emails, cold DMs, YouTube comments. Slower to warm up, but fully in our control - we choose who, when, and how many.

INBOUND

They come to us

Personal brand content, referrals, reputation. Trust is built before the first message - but it takes time to build the audience.

Early on, outbound pays the bills. Inbound is what compounds while you sleep.

14 😣➡️🌟 Pain points → desired outcome → CTA

The three lines that
drive a "yes."

1 · Pain Point

Name the exact frustration: "You're posting weekly but views aren't growing."

2 · Desired Outcome

Paint the result they actually want: "Consistent uploads without touching an editing app."

3 · CTA

Ask for one tiny next step: "Worth a 15-min call this week?"

A message with all three is a pitch. A message missing one is just noise.

15 ⚡ What increases the chance of a faster close

Don't send more messages.
Make each one matter.

1 · Specific observation

Point to something real on their content, website or channel.

2 · Business reason

Connect the observation to time, distribution, consistency, audience or growth.

3 · Tiny next step

Ask for a reply, permission to send ideas, or a short meeting - not a giant commitment.

4 · Proof / specificity

Use relevant work, a concrete idea, or a short teardown when available.

5 · Reduce friction

Make the meeting easy: clear duration, clear reason, clear next step.

6 · Keep momentum

When they reply, respond quickly and move toward one clear next action.

The goal of outreach is usually not “close the deal.” The first goal is to earn the next conversation.

22 💬 Comment rules - get a reply, not a scroll-past

3 sentences. Zero pitch.

✅ Structure

1. Name the exact moment that landed.
2. One line on WHY it's true / the principle behind it.
3. Extend the idea OR ask a sharp, answerable question.

❌ Never

No praising the editing/hooks/B-roll. No mentioning you're an editor. No “great video,” no emojis, no links.
Example comment

Hey Sam, the part where you said most creators quit right before the algorithm "gets" them really stuck with me. That's basically survivorship bias working against people who give up one month too early. Did you track how long it actually took before your first video really took off?

70% of the time, end with a question they'll want to answer. ❓

🇧🇩 Language tip: keep it in simple English - short sentences, everyday words. If a word needs a dictionary, cut it. Simple beats "fancy" every time.

28 🌟 Extra tips that actually move the needle

The small things
that compound.

📋 Track everything

One row per lead: date contacted, channel, last touch, next action due. No memory, only the tracker.

⚡ Reply speed wins deals

The first person to respond fast usually wins the meeting - treat every reply like it's urgent.

🧱 Batch the research

Do all your prospect research in one block, then all your writing in another - don't switch tasks mid-flow.

📩 Paste their reply into the prompt

When using the email generator, feed it the creator's actual reply - it makes the email land noticeably warmer.

🎯 Never stack asks

One clear CTA per message. Two asks in one message means zero gets answered.

📈 Quality over volume, but volume still matters

10 well-researched messages beat 50 generic ones - but 10 has to happen every single day.
28.5 💰 How to actually close more of the calls you book

More calls ≠ more deals.
Better calls = more deals. 📞

You already know how to fill the top of the funnel. These are the moves that raise the % who say yes at the bottom. 👇

🎥 Show, don't tell

A 60-sec Loom beats 500 words every time. Let them SEE the idea on their own content before you ask for anything.

🪞 Mirror their words

On the call, repeat their pain point back in their own words. It proves you listened - trust jumps instantly.

🔢 Anchor with a number

"Creators like you usually see 2-3× more Shorts output" lands harder than "we can help you grow."

🚦 One clear next step

End every call with ONE decision to make - not "let me think" but "yes/no by Friday" or a trial clip.

⏱️ Book the call ON the call

Never say "I'll send some times" - open the calendar together, live, before they hang up.

🧊 Handle "let me think" fast

Ask directly: "Is it price, timing, or trust?" Naming the real objection out loud usually unlocks it.
💡 A confused prospect never says yes. Simplify the offer until a 10-year-old could repeat it back.
💡 Silence after your price isn't rejection - it's thinking. Don't fill it. Let them speak first.
💡 Every "no" without a reason is a leak in your funnel. Always ask why before you let a lead go cold.

Closing isn't convincing. It's removing every reason to hesitate. ✅
